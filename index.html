How It Works
Four simple steps to get your items into your locker.
01🔐
Sign in with Discord
Log in with your Discord account so we know who you are and can DM you updates.
02📝
Submit your ticket
Press Our Items, pick the item you're claiming, and fill in your VRFS ID and message link.
03🔍
Staff review
Our team checks your ticket and either accepts or denies it. You'll get a DM with the result.
04✅
Item sent
Once approved, you'll be told when your item is sent to your dash or locker.
